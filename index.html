About

I am a B.Sc. student in Fundamental Science (Mathematics and Physics) at Dalian University of Technology. My research is on multimodal large language models: making their reasoning more efficient, finding where they fail between seeing and planning, and building agents that act in GUIs and games.

  • Multimodal LLMs / VLMs
  • Efficient Multimodal Reasoning
