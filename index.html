Research Experience

Multimodal Reasoning and Game Agents

Aug 2026 – Present
Research Intern, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology · Advisor: Prof. Shiwen Ni
  • YOTO (first author, CVPR 2027 submission). A method for small hybrid-reasoning VLMs: when a thinking trace is unlikely to finish, it is handed to the same weights’ non-thinking mode to produce the answer, cutting wasted reasoning on multimodal math (MathVision).
  • Sokoban benchmark (co-first author, CVPR 2027 submission). A Sokoban-based benchmark that separates visual perception from multi-step planning, to locate where multimodal LLMs fail between seeing and planning.
  • Latency-robust game agents (ongoing). Studying how inference delay degrades vision-language-action agents in Minecraft (JARVIS-VLA in MineStudio), and developing a self-distillation method where the model’s output on the latest frame supervises the same model given a delayed frame.

Text-to-Motion Generation

Jun 2025 – Present
Research Intern, Multimodal Perception and Interaction Lab, South China University of Technology · Advisor: Prof. Changxing Ding
  • Contributed to a diffusion-based text-to-motion method that jointly denoises motion and inter-joint distances in an asymmetric scheme (ICLR 2027 submission, third author).
  • Explored chain-of-thought reasoning for finer control over numerical instructions, such as how many steps to take or how far to turn.
  • Explored SFT followed by GRPO for step-count following; found low intra-group diversity and inconsistent step annotations in existing motion datasets to be the main obstacles.
  • Explored a Titans-inspired long-term memory mechanism to retain fine-grained constraints in long-text motion generation.

Tool-Using Agents for Omni-Modal Deep Search

May 2026 – Jun 2026
Research Intern, School of Future Technology, South China University of Technology · Advisor: Prof. Xiaowei Hu
  • Built agents for Omni-DeepSearch, a benchmark that requires reasoning over audio, video and web content.
  • Replaced the baseline’s fixed workflow with tools the agent chooses for itself, improving both accuracy and efficiency.
  • For video-grounded questions, explored letting the model decide when to revisit the full video instead of fixed-interval frame sampling.
